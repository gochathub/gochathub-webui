# Notifications

## Turn them on

Open Settings, then Notifications, and switch on Allow Notifications. Your browser asks for permission. Choose Allow.

If you refuse, the switch stays on but nothing appears. Open your browser's site settings for goChatHub, change Notifications to Allow, and switch the setting off and on again.

## While the app is closed

Where the browser supports it, goChatHub registers for push, so a notification arrives even with the window closed. Push needs a secure (HTTPS) connection. On servers without one, or in browsers without push, you still get notifications while a tab is open, including hidden tabs.

Each notification shows the sender, the room and a preview of the message. Selecting it opens that room, reusing an open window when there is one.

The installed app is the most dependable way to receive push. See [Getting started](/help/getting-started).

## The Notifications panel

The Notifications entry in the left bar lists invitations and a few messages about things that went wrong, such as a failed delete. Invitations have Accept and Decline buttons.

Keep Notifications, also in Settings, controls whether these items are saved after they arrive.

## Turning them off

Switch Allow Notifications off. The app unregisters this browser from push, so it stops getting them.
