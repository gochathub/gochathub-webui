# Conversations and groups

## Start a conversation

Open Conversations and press the compose button at the top. The Compose window has two tabs.

- Contact: pick a person to start a direct conversation.
- Group: name the group, press Next, choose the members, then press Finish. You become the group's admin.

## Add a contact

Open Contacts and press the add button. In the Add Contact window, type a username in the search box and select the person from the results. Contacts are people on your own server, so you search by username, not by email.

## The conversation menu

Right-click a conversation in the list to open its menu.

- Conversation info shows members, shared media and, for admins, the editing tools.
- Mark all read appears only when the conversation has unread messages.
- Archive conversation hides it from your list. Unarchive brings it back. Archiving affects only you.
- Leave group removes you from a group. Admins see Delete group (or Delete conversation) instead.

Leaving asks for confirmation. Deleting removes the room for everyone and cannot be undone.

## Roles

A group has admins and members. The person who creates the group is its first admin.

Admins can edit the group's name and avatar, add members, and remove members. Admins can also promote a member to admin or demote an admin to member. In the member list, use the menu next to a name for Promote to admin, Demote to member or Remove from group.

Any member can leave. Only an admin can delete the group.

## Invitations

If someone invites you to a room, it shows up under Notifications with Accept and Decline buttons. Whether people can add you to groups directly is a setting. See [Account and security](/help/account-security).
