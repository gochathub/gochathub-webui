# Appearance and spell check

## Dark mode

Open Settings, then Appearance, and switch Dark Mode on or off. The moon and sun button in the left bar does the same thing.

## Grammar and spelling

Switch on Grammar & Spelling to check your English as you type. The first time, your browser downloads the checker, which is about 16 MB. After that it runs on your device and your text is not sent anywhere to be checked.

Problems get an underline. Click one to see suggestions. You can accept a suggestion, add the word to your dictionary, or ignore it.

The checker skips code, links, mentions, emoji shortcodes and URLs.

Your on/off choice and your dictionary are stored with your account, so they follow you to other browsers. While the checker is on, the browser's own spell check is turned off in the message box so the two do not fight.

The check is English only. It is a writing aid, not a rule: it can be wrong, especially with names and technical words.
