# Read receipts

The small icon beside your own messages tells you how far along they are.

- A clock means the message has not reached the server yet. It is waiting to send.
- One check means the server has it.
- Two checks mean it was delivered.
- Two checks in the read style mean it was read.

## In groups

In a group, the icon summarises everyone. Two checks appear only when every member has the message, and the read style appears only when every member has read it. Until then, the message stays at the earlier step.

## Why a message may stay "delivered"

Reading is shared only if the reader allows it. Under Settings, Privacy and Safety, the Show Read Receipt switch controls whether people can see that you have read their messages. When someone turns it off, your messages to them may stay at "delivered" even though they read them. That is intended, and you cannot override it.

The same switch applies to you. If you turn it off, other people stop seeing when you read their messages.

## Last seen

Show Last Seen controls whether people can see your last login. It is separate from read receipts.
